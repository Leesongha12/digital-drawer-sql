Q09: 집계: 독자별 읽은 분량과 시간

SQL
SELECT reader_id, COUNT(*) AS sessions, SUM(episodes_read) AS episodes, SUM(minutes_spent) AS minutes FROM reading_log GROUP BY reader_id ORDER BY episodes DESC, reader_id;

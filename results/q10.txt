Q10: 집계: 작품별 평균 평점과 평가 수(NULL 제외)

SQL
SELECT novel_id, COUNT(rating) AS rating_count, ROUND(AVG(rating),2) AS avg_rating FROM reading_log GROUP BY novel_id ORDER BY avg_rating DESC, novel_id;
